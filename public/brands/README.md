# Sign-in badge assets

These locally served assets are used only on the simulated demo access screen.

- Google: `google.png`, downloaded from [Google's identity asset](https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png). [Branding guidance](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines).
- Microsoft: `microsoft-source.svg` is the upstream [Microsoft sign-in badge](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/media/howto-add-branding-in-apps/ms-symbollockup_signin_light.svg). `microsoft.svg` contains its four original symbol rectangles with their original colors and geometry, cropped to the symbol. [Branding guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/entra/identity-platform/howto-add-branding-in-apps).
- Apple: `apple.png`, downloaded from the [official logo-only button endpoint](https://appleid.cdn-apple.com/appleid/button/logo?color=black&height=44&width=44&scale=3), including its original padding. [Button guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sign-in-with-apple/).

The logos remain the trademarks of their respective owners. No authentication SDK, provider connection, or real account access is added.
